Why you can trust it

Why does Windows show a warning?

On first launch Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”: it happens with programs that are not signed with a code-signing certificate, which costs money. Free to Race is free and made by one fan in spare time: it is not signed yet.

To open it: More info → Run anyway. Only the first time.

Every release on GitHub has a .sha256 file next to the installer with its fingerprint, to check that your download is the published file (how). The program on your PC counts nothing and sends nothing about you.

Privacy: what it collects

  • No account, no sign-in, no email.
  • No advertising, cookies or tracking: not in the program, not in the app, not on this site.
  • The site and the app count visits with GoatCounter, without cookies and without keeping your IP address. The program on your PC counts nothing.
  • Timing comes straight from the F1 timing feed, not through a server of ours.
